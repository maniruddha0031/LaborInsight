# CMG — labor cost findings, March–October 2026

Generated by the portal's engine from `datasets/cmg-mar-oct-2026.zip` (17 CSV files, 4.64 M rows). The portal recomputes everything on every run; this page is a snapshot of that output plus the analyst's reading of it.

**Scope:**
- **Period:** 2 Mar – 31 Oct 2026 (8 months). March has 30 of 31 days.
- **Coverage:** 121 stores and 3,422 employees (GEID).
- **Labor:** $25.31 M paid across 1.63 M paid hours, against 1.59 M scheduled hours.
- **Excluded:** test store 77777 (placeholder ID, synthetic employee names).

## Leakages discovered (ranked)

1. **Labor above plan is persistent, not random.**
   - Stores paid 96,160 h more than they scheduled on 57% of store-days. That is 6.1% of scheduled hours after netting out cover for absences.
   - 24 stores ranked in the worst quartile for over-plan cost in 5+ of 8 months. Seven of them did so in all 8: 06801, 07408, 07436, 07750, 07883, 08246, 08372.
   - 07750 ran 29.9% over its schedule.
2. **Late clock-outs are the largest controllable leak.**
   - 62,216 h gross; 21,334 h ($340K) beyond the 10-minute tolerance after netting.
   - 52% comes from opening shifts staying past their end. This points to an unscheduled handover between opening and mid shifts.
   - 2,125 employees repeat it in 4+ weeks.
   - Late clock-outs ticked up in October.
3. **Early clock-ins:**
   - 34,953 h gross; $175K avoidable.
   - Concentrated in closing shifts (47%).
   - 1,417 recurring employees.
   - Worsening: up 8% in the last 3 months vs the first 3.
4. **Unscheduled work is 9.6% of paid hours.**
   - Mostly whole days worked by a GEID with no shift on the schedule (24,213 employee-days; 3,579 employee-weeks with zero scheduled hours).
   - About 53,000 h exceed same-day coverage gaps, so they are not replacement cover.
   - Highest on weekends: 10.9% of paid hours on Saturday vs 7.8% on Monday.
   - Improving: −22% over the period.
5. **Overtime premium of $246,672, and 55% of OT hours were written into the schedule.**
   - The published schedule itself put employees over 40 h, accounting for $138K of the premium. That is avoidable by re-scheduling.
   - The top 10% of employees generate 60% of OT hours.
   - 185 OT weeks spanned more than one store.
6. **Manual time entry: 10.0% of paid hours ($3.05 M of pay) were keyed in by managers, not captured by the clock.**
   - 78% were entered more than an hour after the time they record; 21% a day or more later (median 6.5 h).
   - 73% of manual clock-in times are round quarter-hours, vs 11% for device punches.
   - The manual share is **rising** (+32%).
   - Stores 01664, 01674 and 05175 key in a third of their punches.
7. **Edits add paid time.**
   - 3.8% of paid punches were edited.
   - Outside forgotten clock-out corrections, edits add 4× more time than they remove: net +8,872 h.
   - 2,182 edits moved a punch exactly onto the scheduled time.
   - 60% of edited times are round quarter-hours.
8. **Skipped planned meals.**
   - On 52% of shifts with a scheduled unpaid meal, the employee stayed on the clock: 11,524 h paid that was planned unpaid.
   - 84% of 6 h+ shifts have no meal break recorded.
   - 16 stores record virtually none.
   - The clock flags no break violations for production stores, so break rules appear not to be configured.

## Additional intelligence (beyond the brief)

- **Unflagged minors.**
  - All 19,569 punches by the 426 employees under 18 carry Minor = "No", although the schedule flags 424 of them as minors.
  - The clock's minor-law controls therefore cannot apply.
  - 8,551 of those punches ended after 22:00.
- **Self-edits.**
  - 197 users changed their own punches 5,969 times (9.8% of edits). One user's own punches account for 161 of their 198 edits.
  - Editors carry no GEID, so this is a name match to confirm.
- **Shared logins.** 60% of punch changes were made through generic `gm<store>@` accounts, so there is no individual accountability.
- **Biometric punching is effectively absent.**
  - 28 of 482,851 events (0.006%); PIN punches are 91%.
  - No biometric vs non-biometric accuracy claim can be made from this data; a pilot is recommended.
- **Pay-rate anomalies.**
  - 75 punches for 40 employees carry rates more than 3× the employee's own rate, up to $650/h.
  - That is $53.6K above normal rates, probably bonuses or salary keyed as hourly. Leakage is costed at normal rates.
- **OT paid without premium.** 253 OT hours (31 employee-weeks, 10 employees) were paid at straight time or $0. Confirm exemption or pay set-up.
- **Missing Payroll IDs.** 15,691 paid punches (97,444 h, $1.35 M, 724 employees) have no Payroll ID. This is a payroll reconciliation risk.
- **Forecast errors.**
  - Store 06602's forecast was 2.4× actual in May and 5.1× in August.
  - 06769 was 2.3× actual in October.
  - Schedules built on these inflate labor; they also distort forecast accuracy.
- **June 8–16 edit storm.**
  - 1,260 edits on 8 June vs a typical 229 per day, across many stores.
  - The manual-entry share peaked at 15.3% in June.
  - This looks like a clock/system event; time for those days is reconstructed.
- **Sales vs labor (monthly).**
  - Sales achievement 99.3%, SPLH 60.6, labor 25.6% of sales.
  - In 69 store-months (60 stores) sales fell more than 3% while paid hours rose: about 11,165 excess hours (≈$171K estimate, not additive).
  - Against similar-volume peers, 60 stores run below the peer SPLH. This is a low-confidence benchmark.

## What improved / worsened (last 3 vs first 3 months)

- **Improved:**
  - Paid vs scheduled hours (+3.9% → +1.4%)
  - Unscheduled hours (−22%)
  - Avoidable % of labor (−10%)
  - OT hours (−5%)
  - Edit rate (−5%)
- **Worsened:**
  - Manual punch share (+32%)
  - Early clock-ins (+8%)
  - Skipped planned meals (+8%)
- **Stable:** late clock-outs, missed meal records (~84%), labor %.

## Data quality notes that bound these conclusions

- Schedule export: 33.6% exact duplicate rows (up to 10 copies of a shift), removed.
- `Punch Audit.csv` has exactly 500,000 rows, a likely export cap, so manual-entry counts from it are lower bounds. The paid-punch export, which is complete, gives the same manual share (10%).
- Sales files contain 15-minute slots. They are used **monthly only**, as instructed.
- Premium pay rows exist only for the test store, and all misc pay rows are $0.
- Several export terms (GEID, Punch Method "Password", Source Id "CLOCK_TIMECARD") are not product field names. They are interpreted from their values.
